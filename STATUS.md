# Ocean terrain

Larger, deeper oceans and underwater formations await human in-game validation. The default Overworld uses the new basin and seamount profiles. Amplified shares the wider coastline, while Large Biomes uses its own terrain functions.

Across seeds `42`, `2026` and `8675309`, ocean biomes covered 56% of 28,227 sampled positions (vanilla 33%). Mean ocean crossings along the grid axes grew from 530 to 879 blocks. Median sampled ocean depth grew from 15 to 67 blocks. All 346 sampled inland columns with vanilla continentalness of at least 0.30 retained their height and biome.

The build and headless Forge test passed. In a fresh default world on seed `0`, visit `/tp @s -128 50 -1216` in spectator mode. The generated crater floor is at Y −24, with rim points at Y 31–36 and a basin at Y −32 just west of the rim. Inspect the slopes and sail across a widened ocean before marking either backlog item complete.
